## info

Uncredentialed and learning by breaking domains from the ground up. This space ignores generic CTF jeopardy challenges, focusing entirely on mapping and dissecting the fragile architecture of Windows Active Directory.

The approach is shaped by a bookshelf of hard sci-fi, the structural isolation of Le Guin, the moral weight of Karamazov, and Süskind's hyper-focused obsession with invisible traces. Operating outside traditional pipelines and the work happens in the margins: standing up domain controllers, tracing Kerberos edge cases, and turning raw technical friction into intuition.
